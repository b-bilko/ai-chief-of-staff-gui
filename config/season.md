# This Season

A season is three to six months, not a year. It is the honest answer to what
this stretch of your life is for.

## 1. What is this season about?

One or two sentences. Not a goal list, the actual center of gravity. What would
make you say the last few months were well spent?

{{answer}}

## 2. Where should your attention go?

Roughly how you want your energy split across your threads. Rough percentages or
plain ranking, both work. This is what a review compares against when it tells
you a thread went quiet.

{{answer}}

## 3. What should be tracked without you asking?

Things you want surfaced in briefings and recaps even on the days you forget to
mention them. A relationship you keep dropping, a habit you are rebuilding, a
project that stalls when you look away.

{{answer}}

## 4. What are your non-negotiables?

The two or three daily inputs that keep your floor from dropping. Sleep, a walk,
time with someone, hands on real work. Be concrete enough to answer yes or no at
the end of a day.

If you name these, the evening wrap runs a short check on them. If you leave this
blank, it skips the check entirely.

{{answer}}

## 5. One custom question for the evening wrap (optional)

One question you want asked every night for this season, on top of the standard
nine. Make it something you would actually want a month of answers to.

Leave blank to skip.

{{answer}}
